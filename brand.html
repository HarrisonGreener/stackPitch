01 The brand

Own it.
Play it everywhere.

Stack is the home for the records people own. It turns a shelf of vinyl and CDs into a library you can play anywhere, share with family and sell on when you’re done.

WarmSunset colours, wood, paper and analogue texture. Never cold or techy.
CollectorRespects the object: the sleeve, the pressing, the crackle.
PlayfulDJ Selector, stickers and little surprises. Fun, never childish.
HonestYour music is free. We only charge for sharing.

07 Partner lock-ups

Side by side,
as equals.

When Stack works with a record shop, label or event, the two logos sit on one line, matched by visual height, divided by a thin rule.

2x2x
  • Stack comes first on Stack-led work: on the left, or on top when stacked.
  • Equal height. Match the partner’s cap height to the Stack wordmark, never larger.
  • Divider: a 2px rule in Cocoa, with 2x clear space on each side.
  • Partner-led work: the partner leads, and Stack follows with “with Stack” or “Powered by Stack”.
  • Never merge the marks, recolour the partner’s logo or put both inside one shape.
Compact: S mark with partner

13 Selector rules

Use him
like a guest star.

Do
  • Use him for moments: a welcome, a pick, a tip, a celebration.
  • Show him whole, feet and all, sitting on or interacting with real objects.
  • Keep him smaller than the main content.
  • Use one Selector per screen or page.
Don’t
  • Redesign him, change his colours or add a blue headband stripe.
  • Crop off his legs or squash his proportions.
  • Put him on every page or behind body text.
  • Distort his eyes or give him a scary expression.

Layout4px spacing grid. 24px page margins on phones, 1120px max content width on desktop.
ButtonsPills. One primary orange-to-red action per screen; others are tinted or ghost.
Cards20px radius on Paper, a soft 1–3px shadow. No hard borders.
Motion200–400ms with the Stack ease. Records spin at 33⅓. Respect reduced motion.
CopySentence case. No spaced capitals. No numbering in small labels.
