23 Legal, ethics, sustainability

Doing it properly,
from day one.

Music licensing

Stack streams only records you prove you own, under licences from labels and publishers, with royalties on every play. No licence, no launch.

Data and GDPR

Collections, listening and location are personal data: consent, UK storage, export and delete at any time.

Puck safety

UKCA and CE marking, safety testing, and WEEE take-back for old electronics.

Marketplace fairness

Consumer rights on second-hand sales, payment held until the record arrives, honest grading.

Fair to artists

Every play pays royalties, so sharing your records still pays the people who made them.

Sustainability

Selling records on keeps vinyl in use. The Puck has a recycled case, minimal packaging and a repair-first approach.

24 Risks

The risks,
and the answers.

RiskMusic rights

Owning a record doesn’t give you the right to stream or copy it.

AnswerPartner with labels

Many records already come with a download code. Stack becomes its home, licensed, with royalties on every play.

RiskHardware costs

Making, certifying and shipping the Puck is expensive.

AnswerStart small, keep it optional

Barcode and Discogs import work without it. The Puck launches in small batches through record shops.

RiskAn empty marketplace

Buyers won’t come without sellers, and the other way round.

AnswerLaunch city by city

Manchester first, with partner record shops listing second-hand stock from day one.
